2. Chuẩn đầu ra của chương trình đào tạo (Programme's Expected Learning Outcomes) II. Sinh viên tốt nghiệp Cử nhân kỹ thuật ngành Kỹ thuật Hóa dược có các kiến thức, kỹ năng và  năng lực như sau:

Graduates of Bachelor's degree in Chemical Pharmaceutical Engineering must have the following knowledge, skills and competencies as follows:

- 2.1. Kiến thức về lĩnh vực Kỹ thuật Hóa dược

The knowledge in Chemical Pharmaceutical Engineering

- 2.1.1. Khả năng áp dụng kiến thức cơ sở toán và khoa học cơ bản để thiết kế, tính toán một  hệ thống, một phân xưởng hoặc một công đoạn sản xuất sản phẩm dược phẩm; tham  gia tổ chức tiến hành các thí nghiệm, phân tích, xử lý dữ liệu và thực hành.

The ability to apply basic mathematics and basic science to design, calculate a system, a workshop or a production process of pharamaceutical products, to conduct experimental design , data analysis and practice.

- 2.1.2. Khả năng áp dụng các kiến thức cơ sở và cốt lõi của ngành như Hóa vô cơ, Hóa hữu  cơ, Hóa lý, Hóa phân tích, Quá trình và Thiết bị Công nghệ Hóa học để nghiên cứu,  phân tích và thiết kế các quá trình, thiết bị trong lĩnh vực Kỹ thuật Hóa dược.
- The  ability  to  apply  core  engineering  knowledge  in  chemical  engineering,  including inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, physical chemistry, analytical chemistry, process and equipment in chemical engineering, to participate in studying, analyzing and designing processes and equipment in the Chemical Pharmaceutical Engineering field.
- 2.1.3. Khả năng sử dụng các kiến thức chuyên sâu của ngành kết hợp với sử dụng các phương pháp, công cụ hiện đại để tham gia tính toán, thiết kế, mô phỏng, chế tạo và thực hiện các giải pháp công nghệ trong lĩnh vực Kỹ thuật Hóa dược.

The  ability  to  apply  in-depth  chemical  engineering  knowledge  in  combination  with advanced methods and tools to participate in calculating, designing, modeling, manufacturing  and  implementing  technological  solutions  in  Chemical  Pharmaceutical engineering.

- 2.1.4. Khả năng sử dụng thành thạo các kiến thức bổ trợ (Đồ họa và vẽ kỹ thuật, mô phỏng, phương pháp số, …) trong thiết kê, chế tạo và vận hành trong Kỹ thuật Hóa dược.

The ability to proficient use of complementary knowledge, including graphic and technical drawing, simulation, and numerical methods, in design, fabrication and operation in Chemical Pharmaceutical Engineering.

The professional skills

- The ability to present and write professional reports.

- The ability to use English effectively at work, achieve IELTS 5.5 or equivalent.
- 2.2.8. Khả năng phân tích đánh giá, phát hiện các vấn đề thực tiễn và phản biện.

The ability to analyze, evaluate, detect practical problems and critical.

- 2.2.9. Khả năng tư duy hệ thống trong quá trình giải quyết công việc chuyên môn.

The ability to think systematically in the process of solving a professional job.

- 2.2.10. Khả năng phân tích, tổng hợp, đánh giá dữ liệu và thông tin để sử dụng và khai thác các công nghệ một cách hiệu quả.

The ability  to analyze, synthesize, evaluate data and information  to use and exploit technologies effectively.

## I. MATHEMATICS

## 1. MI2026 - Probability and Statistics

Content: Random events and probability calculation, random variables, probability distributions, random vectors, statistical estimation theory, statistical decision theory, correlation, and regression.

Objective: The course provides students with the knowledge of probability such as concepts and inference rules for probability as well as random variables and common probability distributions (one-dimensional and  two-dimensional);  basic  concepts  of  mathematical  statistics  which  help  students  in  dealing  with statistical problems in estimation, hypothesis testing, simple linear regression, and correlation. Through the acquired knowledge, students are given a methodology for approaching practical models and finding an appropriate solution.

## 2. MI1016 - Calculus I

Content: Limits and continuity. The derivative of functions of one variable and applications to related rates, maxima and minima. Transcendental functions. An introduction to the indefinite and definite integrals and areas.  Differentiation  of  functions  of  several  variables,  maxima  and  minima  of  functions  of  several variables. Lagrange's multipliers.

Objective: This is the first course in calculus and analytic geometry. It covers basic notions of functions given in Cartesian coordinate system as well as in polar coordinate including techniques of curve sketching, basic techniques of differentiation and integration with variety applications, and partial derivatives and applications in the domain of functions of several variables.

## 3. MI1026 - Calculus II

Content: Applications  of  differential  calculus,  vectors,  cylindrical  and  spherical  coordinates,  double integrals, triple integrals, line integrals, surface integrals and vector fields.

Objective: This  is  the  second  course  in  calculus.  It  covers  basic  notions  of  multiple  integrals:  double integrals, double integrals in polar coordinates, triple integrals, triple integrals in cylindrical and spherical coordinates, line integrals, surface integrals, with variety applications in vector field studies, in mechanics, in electrics, in physics, etc.

## 4. MI1036 - Algebra

Content: Sets. Mappings. Complex numbers. Matrices. Determinants. Systems of linear equations. Vector spaces.  Linear  maps.  Eigenvalues  and  eigenvectors.  Euclidean  spaces.  Quadratic  curves  and  quadric surfaces.

Objective: This course aims to provide students with basic knowledge of set theory, complex numbers and linear algebra.

## II. BASIC CHEMISTRY

## 5. CH1012E - Chemistry I

Content: The properties and motion characteristics of micro-particles, wave functions and Schrodinger equations. The basic assumptions of multiple electron atom problem follow approximately one electron atom. On the basis of the results of solving the hydrogen atom problem, knowing the shape of s, p, d, f electron  clouds  and  the dependence of energy levels s, p, d, f on the principle quantum number n and secondary quantum number l. Determining the electron structure of the atoms, thereby understanding the physical causes of the periodic law and the principle to build the periodic table, the relationship between the electron structure of atoms of elements and their position and properties in the periodic table. Theories of  chemical  bonding:  Valence  bond  theory  and  molecular  orbital  theory.  Intermolecular  forces  and collecting element states of matter: solid, liquid, gas state.

Objective: This course aims to provide students with fundamental knowledge on the basis of quantum mechanics,  the  electronic  structure  of  atoms  and  molecules,  chemical  bonds,  geometric  structure  of molecules. Upon completion of this course, the students will be able to understand the structure of matter and identify the lawful dependency relationship between properties of substances and their structures.

## 6. CH1015E -  Chemistry II

Content: Chemical thermodynamics: Study on changes in thermodynamic quantities such as ΔH, ΔS, ΔU, ΔG of chemical processes or chemical reactions. Determine the direction of the process. At the same time, determine the equilibrium conditions of chemical systems. Apply the basic principles of thermodynamics to study of reactions and balances in solutions. Since then, applied to the chemical technology processes in practice.

Chemical kinetics: study of reaction rate and reaction mechanism: factors affecting the reaction rate, mass effect law, Van't Hoff rule, Arrehnius equation and empirical method to determine the order of reaction and activation energy.

Electrochemistry:  batteries  and  electrodes:  the  principle  of  converting  chemical  energy  into  electrical energy, electrode potential and electrodes, dimensions and equilibrium of redox reactions.

Objective: Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

Gain the knowledges of the basic concepts and laws of chemistry in the field of chemical thermodynamics, chemical kinetics and electrochemistry and their applications in engineering processes.

On the basis of the knowledge and skills achieved students can solve simple exercises and experiments and apply the basic principles of chemical science to study other subjects, resolve specific tasks in various fields and practical problems.

## 24. CH4517E - Structure-activity relationship

Content: The course will provide knowledge from basic to advance as well as practical examples through recent multidisciplinary research, chemistry, physics, and biology, and computaional theory. The knowledge in  this  subject  centralizes  the  importance  and  applicability  of  the  relationships  between  structure  and activity (QSAR) in chemistry, especially in chemistry and drug discovery. All the lectures are devoted to emphasize the core idea of QSAR: as the molecular structure changes, the activity and properties of the molecules also change. The QSAR model is an important approach in drug discovery that relates molecular structure with biological and pharmaceutical activities. The QSAR methods are based on the calculation and comparison of molecular properties with the purpose of identifying molecules similar to those studied. Compared with traditional pharmaceutical tectonic methods, QSAR methods require significantly lower computation times and therefore, are mainly used as preliminary filters to reduce the number of compounds that can be used. Used for further screening in later drug development stages. These methods are widely used in academia, industry, and research organizations around the world. The course will focus on technical knowledge as well as practical applications through research. multidisciplinary, advanced, and outstanding theoretical calculation software and techniques in the field of chemistry and theoretical physics for the field of modeling the relationship between renewable energy in the country and in the world. Students will have an overview of the use of theoretical calculation methods in the study of molecular structure and the ability to generalize each relationship between chemical structure and biological, chemical, or physical activities

Objective : This course will provide a general introduction and basic knowledge of physical chemistry for students majoring in chemistry, biology, and toxicology. The principles of QSAR and relevant examples for filling data gaps using QSAR models and other estimation methods will be reviewed and discussed.

- -Students  will  be  able  to  apply  the  fundamental  knowledge  gaining  from  multidiscipline  research including  chemistry,  physics,  and  biology  as  well  as  computational  theory.  The  course  focuses  on molecular  descriptors  that  are  representative  of  the  molecular  features  responsible  for  the  relevant molecular activity. Here descriptors are the chemical characteristic of a molecule in numerical form, used for QSAR/QSPR studies.
- -Students will learn to construct the prediction of biological activities of untested or novel compounds

to provide insight into relevant and consistent chemical properties or descriptors (2D/3D) which defines the  biological  activity.  They  also  can  identify  chemical  categories  for  a  chemical  and  analogues, together with their experimental, or estimated environmental, toxic or health effects. Finally, students can report summary information on the validation processes and results of QSAR models for use in various regulatory purposes.

- -Upon completing this course, students will be able to gaining the ability in corporation and teamwork, communication;  and  be  trained  professional  and  personal  attitudes  towards  working  in  industrial sectors.
