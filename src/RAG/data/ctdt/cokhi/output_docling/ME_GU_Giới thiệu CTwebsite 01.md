## ME-GU - Cooperation Training Program between Hanoi University of Science and Technology, Vietnam and Griffith University, Australia

Name of program:

Mechanical Machine Engineering (International cooperation academic programme)

Education level:

Engineer

Major:

Mechanical Engineering

Program codes:

7520130 (Engineering)

Duration:

5 years

Credits in total:

159 credits

## A. Program Goals

Upon  successful  completion  of  the  international  cooperation  training  program  in  Mechanical Engineering, students will achieve:

1. Basic knowledge and skills in mathematics and applied sciences in the fields of mechanics, electricity-electronics,  control,  and  information  technology  to  meet  the  acquisition  of specialized knowledge; Having specialized knowledge to operate, maintain, calculate and design mechanical systems; solve mechanical technical issues.
2. Personal and professional skills suitable to an interdisciplinary working environment, international  integration  and  adapting  to  the  industrial  revolution  4.0;  can  start  a business.
3. Effective communication and teamwork skills in a professional environment and in the community.
4. The ability to formulate ideas, design, implement and operate the system in the context of business and society.

## B. Program Learning Outcomes

On  successful  completion  of  the  programme,  students  will  have  the  following  knowledge  and ability:

1. A  broad  professional  knowledge  to  adapt  well  to  jobs  that  are  related  to  the  mechanical engineering field; focusing on the ability to apply basic knowledge and core knowledge of the field in combination with the ability to use modern tools to operate mechanical engineering systems/processes/products:
2. 1.1  The  ability  to  apply  knowledge  of  mathematics  and  basic  science  to  design  and calculate mechanical systems / processes / products.
3. 1.2 The ability to apply the background knowledge of the field to perform an analysis on mechanical systems / processes / products.
4. 1.3 The ability to apply core knowledge of the field, combined with the capability to exploit and  use  modern  methods  and  tools  to  participate  in  the  design  and  evaluation  of mechanical engineering systems / processes / products.
5. 1.4 The ability to apply core knowledge of the field to explore, design, develop, and lead the operation of mechanical production systems/processes.
2. Professional skills and personal qualities needed to succeed in the career
7. 2.1 Skills in detecting, analyzing and solving problems in mechanical engineering field.
8. 2.2 Skills in systematic and critical thinking.
9. 2.3 Dynamism, seriousness, and perseverance.
10. 2.4 The ability to experiment, explore knowledge, and conduct research and development.
11. 2.5 Ethics and professional responsibilities.
12. 2.6 An understanding of contemporary issues and lifelong learning.
3. Social skills needed to work effectively in multidisciplinary teams and in an international environment
14. 3.1 Skills in collaboration, organization, and teamwork.
15. 3.2  Communication  skills  through  writing,  presentation,  discussion,  effectively  using modern tools and media.
16. 3.3 Skills in using English at work according to the regulations of the university.
4. The ability to participate in the design / construction / development of mechanical systems / processes / products / technical solutions in fields mechanical engineering in current social and eviromental context.
18. 4.1 The awareness of the close relationship and the influence of technology solutions in mechanical engineering to economical, social, and environmental factors in a globalized world.
19. 4.2 The ability to identify problems and to form ideas of technical solutions; the ability to participate in project settings related to mechanical engineering.
20. 4.3 The ability to design mechanical systems / processes / products / solutions
21. 4.4 Ability to implement / manufacture / exploit mechanical systems / processes / products / technical solutions.
5.  Political  qualities,  a  sense  of  service  to  the  people,  and  good  health  to  meet  the requirements of the national construction and defense :
23. 5.1 Political theoretical qualifications under the general regulations program of the Ministry of Education and Training.
24. 5.2  Physical  education  certificate  and  certificate  of  national  defense  and  security according to the regular for the general Bachelor program from the Ministry of Education and Training.

## E. Employment Opportunities afetr Graduation

-  Students have the opportunity to find employment immediately after graduation with an average salary of 10 to 20 million VND/month.
-  Students who graduate in Australia will be awarded a degree by Griffith University and are allow to work in Australia for a minimum of 2 years.

*Source: Australian Government Job Outlook

Typical job positions include:

-  Design engineers, managers, and operators at domestic and international factories, enterprises, and corporations in the field of mechanical engineering.
-  Lecturers, researchers at universities, research institutes, and government agencies.
-  Pursue postgraduate studies and research at universities in Vietnam and abroad.
